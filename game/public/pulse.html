The town works

WorksSilk pledgedReward rate, last 7 days

Rewards come only from the town's fees: 0.12 % of every market trade goes to the treasury; each day 65 % of it goes to the works (capped at 0.15 % of pledged weight a day), 10 % to the tournament purse, 10 % to the Shell Reserve and 15 % is burned. The Shell Observatory also gets 15 % of every tournament entry. No silk is minted for pledges; a season's purse may add a mint bounded by its ceiling.

The stalls

Prices follow stock and demand (relayer/market/prices.js): a good bought empty gets dearer, a flooded one cheaper. A stall that runs out of silk closes: it stops buying and making goods and sells off its shelf.

Hall of Pledges

From stall_of {trader} on chain: players who pledged silk into a stall (invest_stall) keep it trading and share its fees. The investors' names are in the contract's stall_invest events.

What this page does not show

  • Accounts are not people: the 20 trader and 6 clan wallets are the town's scripted traders (rules, not an LLM), run by the owner on testnet.
  • Silk is an in-game testnet currency with no value outside the game; rates are past fee flow, variable, can be zero.
  • Players' own inventories and story progress (not on this contract), and anything the relayer says that the chain does not confirm: such numbers are marked.
