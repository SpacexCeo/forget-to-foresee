The town works

WorksSilk pledgedReward rate, last 7 days

Rewards come only from the town's fees: 0.12 % of every market trade goes to the treasury; each day 65 % of it goes to the works (capped at 0.15 % of pledged weight a day), 10 % to the tournament purse, 10 % to the Shell Reserve and 15 % is burned. The Shell Observatory also gets 15 % of every tournament entry. No silk is minted for pledges; a season's purse may add a mint bounded by its ceiling.
