Economy Lab

A week of the town market in one minute. Twenty scripted traders buy and sell at the prices the town's price rules give from stock and demand (relayer/market/prices.js). This is a model run (tools/town/fast-week.mjs), not chain data; the live counters are on Town Pulse.

GoodsStallOn the shelfPrice, silkThe trader says

Rules in one line each: every stall makes its goods hourly (faster when they sell dear, slower when cheap); price = base × (120 / stock)^0.4 × demand, between half and twice the base; buyers pick dear goods less and cheap ones more; a stall under 40 silk closes until it sells its way back above 120. Silk is the town's in-game currency, testnet, no value outside the game.
