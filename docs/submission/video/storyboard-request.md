# Storyboard request (for ChatGPT image generation)

Paste everything below the line into ChatGPT. It asks for a storyboard of the submission video, one frame per shot,
in the game's look. The frames are a guide for the shot list and the in-game camera, not footage.

---

You are a storyboard artist for a browser 3D game called "Forget to Foresee". Make a storyboard of a 90-second
trailer: 12 frames, 16:9, one image per frame, consistent characters and style, with a short caption under each
(shot size, camera move, what happens, on-screen text if any).

**Style:** stylised semi-realistic 3D, warm low sun, soft mist, painterly light, cinematic framing. Palette: amber
sunlight, pearl, slate stone, teal accents. No logos, no real brands, no text in the image except where a caption says
"on-screen text".

**Characters:**
- Iris: a young woman, dark hair in a high ponytail, black cropped sports top with a white stripe on the sleeves, black
  leggings, white sneakers; calm, determined.
- Elise: a young woman with dark curly hair up, a long black halter dress with a high slit, red wrist cuffs; graceful.
- Townsfolk: a hill town of half-timbered houses and stone stairs; an elder (a grown man, not old), a guard, an
  innkeeper, a lamplighter, a shardkeeper.
- The pearl dragon: slender, pearl-white iridescent scales, translucent wings with golden veins, a saddle.
- The Hollow Tide: a colossal stone-and-crystal sea serpent with a crystal crown and a glowing cyan core.

**Shots:**
1. A dim modern lab at night, a city skyline in the window. A device "baking nanoparticles" glows on a desk; Iris
   leans over it. Close-up, slow push in.
2. The device flares white; the room dissolves into light. Medium shot, camera shake.
3. Iris wakes on cobblestones in a sunny hill town; a townsperson offers a hand. Low angle, warm light.
4. Tea at the inn: the elders tell her the town on the mountain keeps the world in balance, and ancient scrolls show
   her arrival. Medium two-shot, scroll with a drawing of Iris on the table.
5. The town square: townsfolk trading at stalls, a trading board with live numbers; on-screen text "20,000 real
   transactions a day on NEAR testnet". Wide establishing shot, crane down.
6. Iris fights stone spiders on a green hillside outside the walls; a glowing chest drops. Dynamic action, low camera.
7. Iris finds the Loomblade, a sword of woven golden threads, on a marked stone. Hero close-up, light catches the blade.
8. A cliff stone at sunset; the pearl dragon lands, wings spread; Iris walks towards it. Wide shot, silhouette.
9. Flying on the dragon above a sea of clouds at low sun. Over-the-shoulder from behind and above the rider.
10. The Glass Shoal: a vast mirror-wet salt flat, tall thin blade-shaped crystal spires, broken monoliths; the Hollow
    Tide rises out of the mirror, reflected perfectly. Wide shot, the serpent towering.
11. The fight: Iris with the Loomblade dodges a glowing ground ring, Elise hovers and throws fire orbs. Action shot.
12. Back in town: Iris inserts the last chip into a brass teleport ring, waves goodbye, and vanishes in light; she lands
    on another island: "I'm not home?" On-screen text: "Thank you for playing. Enter your NEAR mainnet address for the
    whitelist at the official launch." Final wide shot.

Give the 12 frames in order with their captions. Keep both heroines' outfits and faces the same in every frame.
