# Demo video and trailer practice for a hackathon game

Read on 2026-11-08 (UTC). Juror notes feeding `docs/jury/rubric.md`.

## Summary for the rubric
- The hackathon's own rule set is the binding one: lead with the user problem, show one complete user action to a visible result, explain NEAR's role concretely, give a next step; a video is required when the demo is local-only (Q-012). https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md (read 2026-11-08).
- Gameplay in the first seconds, no logo or title intro: Derek Lieu ("the first 6 seconds of a video are critical"), Chris Zukowski ("Show gameplay in the first 5 seconds"), Kert Gartner ("Don't make a boring or drawn out intro"). All read 2026-11-08.
- Length: trailer craft says under 90 s unless expert (Lieu), 1 to 1.5 min with 2 min max (Gartner); hackathon demo norms say 2 to 4 min with narration (ETHGlobal LFGHO, AI Tinkerers guide). NEAR sets no length. A 2:00 to 2:20 cut satisfies both camps and the X free-tier cap of 140 s (secondhand, see Findings).
- Narration beats music-plus-text: ETHGlobal rejects "music with text on the video describing your project (instead of talking)" and sped-up footage; RevenueCat: "proof first, polish second".
- Show the product working, not slides: NEAR "Skip long intros, architecture slides, and setup"; AI Tinkerers "show actual functionality, not just slides"; hackathon.com "immediately demonstrating your project".
- "Do the last thing first" (Kawasaki via MaRS): open on the payoff moment (the verified reveal), then show how the player got there.
- Keep the HUD on; viewers read genre and state from it (Lieu, Simplest Trailer). The proof line is HUD text, so it doubles as evidence.
- Honesty: a trailer must match the game (Gartner); for this project that means "checked against the root", never "on-chain", until the contract is deployed (README real/mock table).
- Structure: cold open, introduction, escalation, climax, button, with "accents" so the piece is not "one long montage" (Lieu, Rising Action).
- The project's video is not shot yet (shot list only, `docs/submission/video-shot-list.md`); a juror can score the plan and the X-post draft, not the video.

### Derek Lieu, The First Shot of the Game Trailer (2022-08-01)
- Source: https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2022/8/1/the-first-shot-of-the-game-trailer (read 2026-11-08)
- "the first 6 seconds of a video are critical to getting people to watch the rest"
- Four openers: a "Basic Game Loop Oner" (one complete loop as a mini three-act story), story-rich setting, "Gameplay Grab Bag", dramatic dialogue; the common rule is "showing player interaction AS SOON AS POSSIBLE".
- "A bad version of this shot would be a random chunk of gameplay which is difficult to parse"

### Derek Lieu, No Logo (2019-01-19)
- Source: https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2019/1/19/no-logo (read 2026-11-08)
- "In general I tell people to never start the trailer with logos (unless of course they're legally obligated to)."
- "I still think the safest approach is to show gameplay first, and shuffle through logos as quickly as possible"
- "Logos have little to no impact on a trailer, if no feelings or memories are evoked upon seeing them."

### Derek Lieu, How to Think About Pacing in a Trailer (2020-11-16)
- Source: https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2020/11/16/how-to-think-about-pacing-in-a-trailer (read 2026-11-08)
- "I usually tell them to make it less than 90 seconds unless you really know what you're doing"
- "by not dwelling too long on any one particular idea, this trailer is always staying fresh"
- Music should have "a nice dramatic arc and a lot of variety"; looping game music rarely does.

### Derek Lieu, The Trailer Line of Rising Action (2018-09-16)
- Source: https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2018/9/16/the-trailer-line-of-rising-action (read 2026-11-08)
- Sections: cold open, introduction, escalation, twist, climax, button; each must stand on its own.
- "Accents are those peaks on the line; they keep the trailer exciting as it builds to the climax."
- "A trailer that feels like one long montage is dull and monotone"; "The keys to making accents work are clarity and context."

### Derek Lieu, The Simplest Trailer to Make For Your Steam Page (2021-04-18)
- Source: https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2021/4/18/the-simplest-trailer-to-make-for-your-steam-page (read 2026-11-08)
- "Show the player DOING stuff as much as possible!"; keep HUD and UI on, viewers identify the genre from them.
- "I'd recommend making the trailer 90-180 seconds long to accommodate clicking through"
- "I would make each shot on the longer side, maybe 3-5 seconds each."
- "don't worry about tightly edited dramatic trailer structure, match cuts, fancy transitions, title cards, or voiceover."
- Leave out logos, slow cinematic pans and shots with no player interaction; many viewers never turn sound on.

## Checklist
1. Gameplay, not a logo or title card, is on screen within the first 5 seconds. (Lieu No Logo; Zukowski 2025, read 2026-11-08)
2. The user problem is stated in one plain sentence inside the first 15 seconds, understandable to a stranger. (NEAR HACKATHON.md; YC Ralston, read 2026-11-08)
3. One complete user action runs uncut in logic (stake, trials pass, reveal) and ends in a visible result held on screen for 3 s or more. (NEAR HACKATHON.md; Lieu Simplest Trailer 3 to 5 s per shot, read 2026-11-08)
4. NEAR's role is explained concretely and honestly: what the contract does, its tested/deployed status; no "powered by NEAR", no "on-chain" claim for undeployed code. (NEAR HACKATHON.md; README real/mock table, read 2026-11-08)
5. The product is shown running (browser, terminal, tests); no architecture slides, no "coming soon" features. (NEAR HACKATHON.md; AI Tinkerers; ETHGlobal LFGHO, read 2026-11-08)
6. Narration carries the explanation; music, if any, sits under the voice; no music-plus-text replacement, no TTS voice. (ETHGlobal LFGHO; Gartner capture with music off, read 2026-11-08)
7. Captions are burned in, because feeds autoplay muted and many viewers never enable sound. (Lieu Simplest Trailer, read 2026-11-08)
8. Length 2:00 to 2:20 for the X cut (free tier 140 s); never speed up footage to fit; cut instead. (ETHGlobal LFGHO; X limits secondhand, read 2026-11-08)
9. Export 1080p at 30 fps (60 fps master optional), at least 720p, under 512 MB; HUD kept on. (ETHGlobal LFGHO; X limits; Lieu Simplest Trailer, read 2026-11-08)
10. The post gives a next step: live link, public repo, one specific question, "built at NEAR HACKATHON", @NEARProtocol and #NEARHackathon in the first post; video attached, not linked. (NEAR HACKATHON.md, read 2026-11-08)
