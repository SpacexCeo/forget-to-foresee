## Summary for the rubric
- NEAR rules: lead with the user problem, show one complete action to a visible result, explain NEAR's role concretely, give a next step; video required for a local-only demo (nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md, read 2026-11-08).
- Trailer craft: gameplay in the first seconds, no logo intro, under 90 s unless expert (derek-lieu.com, read 2026-11-08).

## Findings
### NEAR Hackathon 2026 rules (HACKATHON.md)
- Source: https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md (read 2026-11-08)
- "Lead with the user problem. Make the first line understandable to someone who doesn't know your project."
- "Show one complete moment. In the demo, go from a real user action to a visible result."
- "Skip long intros, architecture slides, and setup."
- "Explain NEAR's role concretely. Say what NEAR enables in this product"; avoid a vague "powered by NEAR".
- "Give people a next step. Link the demo and public repo, and ask a genuine question or invite specific feedback."
- "Q-012. Local-only demo OK? Yes: public repo, reproducible README, X post with a video;"
- "screenshots are not enough when judges cannot open the demo."
