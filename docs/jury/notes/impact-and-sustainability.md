# Impact and sustainability: the "after the hackathon" answer (C5)

Research cycle 7, 2026-11-08 23:18 UTC. Search-result snippets read, not full pages. Moderate depth.

## Findings
1. **Investors' scepticism is about follow-through.** Few hackathon winners stay active; the claim of "under 5%
   active after three months" appears only in a marketing blog and is unverified.
   - https://research.4pillars.io/en/data/content/the-paradigm-shift-of-web3-hackathons (2026-11-08): asks how
     many large-prize winners since 2022 are still active; the problem is not limited to crypto.
   - https://www.chainscorelabs.com/blog/crypto-marketing-and-narrative-economics/bear-vs-bull-marketing/the-future-of-developer-marketing-beyond-the-hackathon-bubble
     (2026-11-08): the under-5% figure, no primary report traced.
   - https://taikai.network/blog/web3-prototype-to-product (2026-11-08): the post-hackathon stage is where builders
     stall (vendor with its own accelerator).
2. **The token must serve the game, not define it.** Play-to-earn economies failed when acquisition stopped:
   https://www.blockworks.com/news/play-to-earn-gaming-is-dead (2026-11-08; AXS down over 95%);
   https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/?p=41947 (2026-11-08; web3 games should be measured on D1 to D30 retention like
   any game); https://0xplay.substack.com/p/4-sustainability-and-shortcomings (2026-11-08; many sinks do not save an
   economy whose structure needs inflows). Single-source numbers (retention falling from over 80% to under 10% with
   the token price) not used.
   Consequence for us: the project has no token, which is an asset in front of this jury if the pitch says so
   ("no token to dump; the economy is agents and memory, the chain only notarises the season").
3. **Follow-on funding path:** a March 2026 secondary guide (https://agentrel.vercel.app/skills/grants/overview-near,
   2026-11-08) lists "NFT / Gaming / Social Applications" as a NEAR Foundation grant area with the portal at
   near.foundation/grants. No NEAR hackathon-to-grant pipeline was found this cycle; not verified on the portal.

## C5 impact checklist (what earns the 7 to 10 step)
1. A named first audience (who plays a 10-minute prophecy season and where they are found).
2. A retention reason in game terms (a new committed season each week; memories carried across seasons).
3. A stated position on tokens: none, or why one would not break the economy.
4. One dated next step tied to NEAR (mainnet deploy of the commit contract, a grant application, a season schedule).
5. One honest metric to watch (D7 return, seasons completed), not a token price.
Three of five present moves C5 from 7 to 8; all five, with the originality bar met, allows 9 to 10.
