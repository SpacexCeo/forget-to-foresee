# Request for the GPT chat: town storyboards (T-001, A-001)

The owner pastes the block below into the ChatGPT session. Returned images go into `docs/art/town/refs/`
named `T-001_<nn>_<what>.png` / `A-001_<nn>_<what>.png`; the lead session passes them to the worker.

---

You are the visual reference artist for our browser 3D game "Forget to Foresee" (Three.js, third-person
camera). A Blender artist will build from your sheets, so clarity beats painterly mood. Task IDs: T-001
(town), A-001 (altar). Everything must be an original design: do not imitate any existing game, film,
town or screenshot.

The world: Iris, a young woman (agent), starts in a compact hill town on the ground. In its square stands
the Tournament altar; registering there lifts her to a floating archipelago of clan camps and four huge
seashell-shaped "Shells" visible high in the sky above the town. The aliens speak in round signs: rings
made of four kinds of strokes (arc, spike, loop, drop). Warm amber = a human decision.

Town look: terraced ground, a shrine on the highest terrace, steps and ramps, light plaster walls, dark
timber frames, stone bases and retaining walls, warm terracotta/amber roofs, carved ring signs on lintels
and banners. Stylized PBR, readable shapes, not photoreal. Daylight, soft haze; the archipelago and Shells
are faint shapes in the sky.

Please produce, one image each, consistent with each other (same buildings, same layout):

1. T-001_01_plan: top-down plan of the town, about 120 × 120 m, with a scale bar: lower gate (spawn), main
   street climbing to the square, the altar in the square, the shrine above it, terraces, edges closed by
   walls, cliffs or houses. Label each area.
2. T-001_02_spawn: eye-level view from the lower gate up the main street (camera about 3 m behind and
   above a 1.7 m character).
3. T-001_03_square: the square with the altar in the foreground and the shrine behind it.
4. T-001_04_kit: a model sheet of the modular house kit in orthographic front/side views: wall bays,
   timber frame patterns, 3 roof types, doors (2.1–2.4 m), windows, chimneys, balconies, awnings, with a
   1.7 m human silhouette for scale.
5. T-001_05_props: a props sheet: well, market stall, crates, barrels, street lamp, banner, planters, trees.
6. A-001_01_altar: the Tournament altar as an orthographic turnaround (front, side, top, 3/4): a round
   stone dais with three steps, a ring-shaped altar stone carved with a ring of round-sign strokes, an amber
   core that glows when someone approaches. Show the human silhouette for scale and mark the glowing parts.
7. T-001_06_palette: a palette and material swatch sheet (plaster, timber, stone, roof tiles, cobbles,
   amber glow, sky haze) with hex values.

For each image add a short note listing any dimensions and choices the modeller must follow. If two of
your images disagree, say which one is the main one.
