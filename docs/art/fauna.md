The only shipped file is `game/public/assets/vfx/glyphmote_formation.json`, generated by `python3 art/fauna/formation.py`.
Until the glbs exist, the code must not reference them as final art. Build: `art/fauna/run_all.sh` (Blender 4.5, Node 20+, gltf-transform 4, toktx 4).

Art direction: the Guests' language (nacre-crystal bodies, sail membranes with radiating spines and Voronoi veins, iridescence)
plus the owner's style addendum. The creatures should feel ancient and unknowable: weathered nacre with crazing and lichen, frayed
and torn membranes, slow majestic motion, a restrained palette with gold bioluminescence. The originals are our own; nothing is copied.

Common contract (planned): glTF Y-up, metres, front +Z (the direction of travel), one skinned mesh per armature, clips at 30 fps.
Loop clips repeat their first pose on the last key, so `LoopRepeat` is seamless (post_fauna.mjs `check` measures this).
Play each instance with a random time offset. Fauna is visual only and never touches game state.

## Glyph motes and published signs
`game/public/assets/vfx/glyphmote_formation.json` (version 1) holds everything the instanced runtime needs:
- `pool` = 96 motes per Shell with fixed `roles` index ranges: ring 0–39, slot0–slot3 (9 motes each) 40–75, dots 76–78, key ring 79–94, spare 95.
- Formation frame: x right, y up, the sign faces +z. One unit = the ring radius. Multiply by `scale_m` (3 m) and place it at `center_m` (0, 0, 13) in the Shell's local frame (Shell local +Z faces the plaza), so the ring hangs 13 m in front of the Shell, facing the plaza.
- The geometry matches the prototype glyph (`prototype-2d/index.html`, `FORM_PATH`). `stroke_templates_slot0[form]` holds 9 `[x, y, angle]` points for slot 0 (top). Rotate them by −p·90° (clockwise) for slot p. Dots: `n` dots at x = (k − (n−1)/2)·`dot_spacing`, y = 0. The key ring (16 motes) is used only for the key sign (double ring). Motes the sign does not use go to `reserve[i]` (a loose halo just behind the ring).
- `angle` is the in-plane direction of the mote's long axis (local +X).
- `example.targets` is a full 96-entry target list for forms [arc, spike, loop, drop], 1 dot, no key (`[x, y, z, angle, used]`).
- `orbit`: per-mote band, phase, radius, bob phase and spin laps. Position: rotate the circle point (r cos θ, 0, r sin θ) with θ = phase + 2π·laps·t/24 by the band `incline_deg` around x, then by `node_deg` around y. Add y += 0.45·sin(2π·3t/24 + bob_phase). Spin the mote around its long axis by 2π·spin_laps·t/24. This is exactly periodic over 24 s.
- `assemble`: mote i keeps orbiting until `delays_s[i]` (the ring sweeps clockwise from the top, then the strokes in reading order, then the dots and the key ring). It then flies 1.6 s on a cubic Bezier: p0, then p0 + v̂·0.3d + up·0.35d, then target + n·2 m, then the target (n = sign normal, +z), with easeInOutCubic and orientation slerp. It finishes with a damped settle of 0.06 m along n. Total 4 s.

Game events → fauna:
| Event | Response |
|---|---|
| A sign is published (prophecy revealed / logogram shown at Shell N) | That Shell's motes `assemble` into the sign's formation (forms and dots from the engine event). Hold for 6–10 s with a slow ring rotation of 6°/s, then disperse back to the orbit (reverse the flight over 2 s). Its jellies speed up (timeScale 1.6) for 3 s. |
| Key sign / double ring | Same as above, with the key ring motes used. |
| Gift at Shell N | Jellies around Shell N brighten (emissive ×1.5, 2 s ease) and rise 1 m. |
| Storm at Shell N | Motes scatter outward to r + 4 m for 3 s. Jellies sink 1 m and dim. Skyrays avoid that Shell's sector for the rest of the tick. |
| Tension ≥ 70 | Skyrays bank away from the archipelago and the whale submerges (y −55). Fewer motes glint (orbit timeScale 0.5). |
| Shells leave (outcome `gone`) | Motes and jellies follow their Shell upward and fade. Skyrays scatter. |
| Player near the Moss camp | The critters hide (see the table). |
| Reduced motion | Freeze the orbits and the pulse at a pose, skip assemble flights (snap the motes to the formation with a 0.5 s fade), keep fauna counts low. |
