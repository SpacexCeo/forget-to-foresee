## Top five threats

For each: what they actually do best, and the specific move that makes us clearly better on that axis. We are not trying to out-Crownfall Crownfall in two days.

### 1. Crownfall — quality, fun, and real on-chain play (Games)

- **They do best:** A walkable 3D three.js realm a judge can finish in ten minutes without a wallet. Google or name login funds a real testnet account, hall rounds burn NEP-245 house coins, races settle in the contract, and the README is already written as do-X-see-Y. Live at [crownfall.nerom.site](https://crownfall.nerom.site); contract claimed as `crownfall-vael.testnet`. Repo: [neromtoobad/crownfall](https://github.com/neromtoobad/crownfall). Demo post: [x.com/neromtoobad/status/2119855648150339694](https://x.com/neromtoobad/status/2119855648150339694).
- **Our move:** Do not clone mint-and-burn halls. Own a different 3D fantasy they do not have: Arrival mystery, a forget-a-memory, a shard that is a biography. Make `?demo=judge` as tight as their ten-minute table (P0), and make the forget + card moment more specific than “burn 400 coin.” They are not on the NFTs track — that lane is still open if the shard is unavoidable and honest.

### 2. Lunearcy — Games-track economy, invisible chain

- **They do best:** The official Games example, shipped. Lucy the merchant is the contract, shelves are shared NEP-245 balances, a week of market can be replayed in a minute, and players never see a wallet. Live at [lunearcy.netlify.app](https://lunearcy.netlify.app); contract claimed as `lunearcy-mw6wrkww.testnet`. Repo: [vertum111/lunearcy](https://github.com/vertum111/lunearcy). Demo post: [x.com/playlunearcy/status/2120063609078153274](https://x.com/playlunearcy/status/2120063609078153274).
- **Our move:** We will not build a second merchant sim before freeze. Make silk → stake → forget → auction the economy that is the gameplay, taught in the three-NPC town quest (P1-B). Keep the chain invisible during play (we already have no wallet popup) and reveal it once, as explorer links on the shard card, so the bonus “players never notice the chain” still holds until the payoff.

### 3. Afterlight — NFTs that change in play, both tracks

- **They do best:** A live command deck with dynamic NEP-171 equipment, gasless NEP-366 sessions, and a 7-day fast-forward a judge can click. Hosted and documented on both of our tracks: [near-afterlight.vercel.app](https://near-afterlight.vercel.app). Repo: [GGGODLIN/near-afterlight](https://github.com/GGGODLIN/near-afterlight). Demo post: [x.com/GGGODLIN/status/2119268196062798316](https://x.com/GGGODLIN/status/2119268196062798316).
- **Our move:** A shard (and, if the owner deploys it, the agent-card) must visibly change with play: forgotten memory, biography, Merkle leaf, auction buyer. Their NFT is equipment that mutates. Ours is a memory that can exist only because it was forgotten, bound to a season root committed before the first move — unique, but only if every player actually sees the card (P1-C, optional P1-D).

### 4. Nearverse — fun in a 3D island, one-click play

- **They do best:** A hosted 3D island you can drive, hunt, haul and sell on, with `nearverse-game-v3.testnet` named in the page and a social-login pitch. A Games judge can open it immediately: [nearverse-murex.vercel.app](https://nearverse-murex.vercel.app/). Demo post: [x.com/gabquelyn/status/2118602977363784094](https://x.com/gabquelyn/status/2118602977363784094).
- **Our move:** Our island, town and Shell have to feel finished, not greybox (P0-A streaming and interior alignment). The town quest gives a human reason to walk — not just haul crates — and leads into a ritual no truck game has.

### 5. Bazaar Sovereign — with Satchel / Deed / OneUp on the NFT axis

- **They do best:** Bazaar is a playable 30-day market where each merchant is pitched as an NFT that owns a wallet — the NFT-track example almost verbatim, plus a Games loop: [bazaar.reconscribe.com](https://bazaar.reconscribe.com). Demo post: [x.com/ReconScribe/status/2118951990667710788](https://x.com/ReconScribe/status/2118951990667710788). Satchel ([christu117.github.io/satchel](https://christu117.github.io/satchel/)), Deed ([ClaudeOpusPremiumAgent/deed](https://github.com/ClaudeOpusPremiumAgent/deed)) and OneUp ([oneup.markets](https://oneup.markets); Life NFTs that claim a cut of house profit; the site displayed 501 Life NFTs earned, not audited here) are cleaner “NFT that does something” specialists.
- **Our move:** Do not fake sub-account wallets or player mints. Win with an NFT that does a different real thing: a memory bound to a public committed season, with Nearblocks links when it is the judge token, and a clearly labeled Unminted preview when it is not. Honesty is the move; a false “Minted on NEAR” toast would lose this axis.

## Where we are clearly differentiated

- **Tone and world.** Almost every strong Games entry is a market town, a cozy sim, or a memecoin minigame. Nobody else is shipping an Arrival-like 3D mystery with a Shell interior and a language of signs. That is the quality gap we can still widen.
- **The NFT's job.** The field is crowded with “NFT that is a wallet.” Our shard is a forgotten memory, bound to a Merkle leaf of a season whose future was committed before play. That is rarer — and still testnet-real for the three judge tokens.
- **Invisible chain, already.** The judged path has no wallet selector and no player signature. Several track winners will lean on social login; we already get the “never notice the chain” bonus if the payoff is a quiet explorer link, not a popup.
- **Honesty, if we keep it.** Many posts are truncated, track-sprayed, or host-light. A README real/mock table, a 5-minute judge path that actually works, and no fake tournament or fake mint will read as adult next to that field. We do not currently have public usage; inventing it would throw this away.
- **What we do not have, and must not pretend to.** No player-owned mint, no NPC wallets, no live weekly tournament, no mainnet, no hosted agent API. Crownfall and Lunearcy are ahead on a living on-chain economy. Catching that before 13 Nov 20:00 Kyiv is not honest or feasible.

## Data gaps

- The public catalog API does not include repository URLs or idea text — only name, tracks and `demo_url`. Repos were found only when a post, site or search revealed them.
- Nearblocks returned 403 from this environment. Contract accounts named in READMEs and HTML were not re-fetched on an explorer.
- Demo videos were not watched. Several important posts are truncated on X (Nims, Farmverse's play URL, Worldo, DiveMarket, cozy hood trails, KEYFALL, and others). Some play URLs may exist in a thread or t.co that did not expand.
- Competitor games were not playtested in a browser beyond HTTP and first-paint HTML. Crownfall, Lunearcy, Afterlight, Nearverse and others deserve a real play pass if time allows.
- Calydon's README play link ([ianlyoo.github.io/calydon/status/](https://ianlyoo.github.io/calydon/status/)) was found late and not HTTP-checked. Farmverse's guest play URL was never recovered. Nims has no found repo or site.
- Usage is almost never independently visible. X impressions/likes measure attention. OneUp's on-site counters were not audited. We have no public FTF usage either.
- The field is still moving. 49 of 70 Games/NFTs rows appeared after 7 Nov, and several landed on the morning of 11 Nov. Recheck the catalog before the 13 Nov freeze and again before submit.
- Official scoring weights and tie-breaks for this event are still unpublished.
