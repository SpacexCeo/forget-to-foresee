# Ambient music candidates (owner review)

Music we may redistribute and modify in this public repository, with credits: Scott Buckley's library and Kevin MacLeod's incompetech catalogue, both Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. No famous copyrighted tracks and no unlicensed remixes.

Listen to [`music_demo.ogg`](music_demo.ogg) and pick one track per scene (for example: `town_night 2, rain 1, archipelago 3, combat 1`). A two-note chime starts each scene; then 1-4 beeps announce the candidate, which plays a 40 s excerpt (2 s fades), loudness-matched to -20 LUFS.

After the picks: each track is cut into a seamless loop, the beds crossfade by zone, weather and combat, duck under the sound effects and follow the music slider; the credits go into `docs/art/manifest.md` and the README.
