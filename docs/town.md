# The town on the ground (client)

Play starts in a hill town below the archipelago. Iris walks from the lower gate up the main street to the
Tournament altar, registers, and is carried up to the archipelago, where the season starts exactly as before.
Art brief and acceptance: `docs/art/town-brief.md`. This page is the code side.

No rule changes: `engine.js`, the room and the parity tests are untouched. The town runs before the play layer
exists; it has no world HUD, no hub and no hints.

## Modules

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `game/src/scenes/town-data.js` | Generated by `art/town/` (Blender plan). Town-local metres: `spawn`, `altar {position, stand, focus, radius}`, `route`, `killY`, `pieces [{name, kind, position, center, radius}]`. Never edited by hand. |
| `game/src/scenes/town-layout.js` | Pure helpers over the data: `TOWN_ORIGIN` (`[0, 1200, 0]`), world-space spawn, altar, route, kill height, fog and mist levels, `altarWake`, `inAltarRange`, the flat dev ground. Node-tested. |
| `game/src/scenes/town.js` | `createTownScene({scene, streamer, flat, pointLights})`: its own `CollisionWorld`, one `PropInstance` per piece on the shared `AssetStreamer`, `ready`, the altar core glow, lamp lights, `groundAt`, `setActive`. |
| `game/src/scenes/town-flow.js` | `createTownFlow(...)`: the objective line, the altar prompt, the registration sequence and its cover, the QA route walker. |
| `game/src/boot-world.js` | Decides with `townPlan`, places Iris in the town, holds her until there is ground, routes input, and on leaving restores the island world, kill height, camera and atmosphere, then calls `startPlay`. |
| `game/src/ui/loading.js` | `townPlan(params)`: the URL rule (pure, tested). |
| `game/src/play/iris-avatar.js`, `game/src/play/gait.js` | Iris's model, gait and look-at, split out of `play/agents.js` so she walks the town before the season. The same `IrisAvatar` is handed to `startPlay` (`options.iris`), so her model loads once. |

The town lives in the same `THREE.Scene` as the archipelago, 1200 m above it (the camera far plane is 500 m), as
the Shell interior does at `[0, 300, 0]`. `player.world` and `rig.world` point at the town's collision world
while Iris is there. `CharacterController.killY` is absolute, so it is `TOWN_ORIGIN.y + TOWN_DATA.killY` in the
town and the island's `KILL_Y` afterwards; the last safe point is always inside the town.

## URL parameters

| URL | Result |
| --- | --- |
| *(none)*, `?demo=judge` | Lobby, then the town. This is the default and the jury link. |
| `?skip=town` | Straight to the archipelago. |
| `?play=direct`, `?cinematic=…`, `?view=…`, `?film=…` | Dev and QA deep links: the archipelago as before, no town. |
| `?play=direct&town=1` | The direct QA link into the town (also with `demo=judge`). |
| `?town=altar` | Spawn at the altar stand, facing the altar (screenshots). |
| `?town=flat` | No models: a flat dev ground and a code-drawn altar. Combine as `?town=altar,flat`. |

A phone (coarse pointer) has no lobby and starts in the town directly.

## Flow

1. `createWorld` builds the town when `townPlan` says so, teleports Iris to the spawn and sets a close street
   camera behind her (`TOWN_CAMERA`: 6.5 m, about 11 degrees above the horizontal, tilt allowed below the
   horizontal to look up at the islands in the sky, far plane 900 m; the island rig values return on leaving).
   The archipelago's models are not loaded: they are out of the frustum and the streamer ignores them.
2. There is no ground until `town_terrain` has streamed in. Iris is held (no physics steps) and the objective line
   reads "The town is taking shape…". When the terrain is real, a ray cast from the spawn finds the ground and she
   is set on it.
   The loading screen (`ui/loading.js`, the gate in `main.js`) stays up until the first town view is complete:
   every street piece real and warmed through `render/warmup.js` (the light `_m` files first), ground under
   Iris, her model in; its ring shows the real bytes of the town pieces. Cap: 30 s, then the town appears as it
   is and the line above covers the rest. When the player leaves the lobby before that, the same loading screen
   comes back (`reopen`) instead of an empty street.
3. The player walks with the normal input: keyboard, click or tap to move, gamepad, Shift or the touch toggle to run.
   The objective reads "Walk to the Tournament altar". Iris's model and gait come from `IrisAvatar`.
4. Inside `altar.radius` of the altar the prompt "Register for the Tournament" appears: key `E`, the gamepad's
   interact button, or a tap on the prompt (it is a button; on touch it shows no key cap).
5. Registration (`TOWN_TRANSITION`, about 3 s): Iris is held, the amber core flares and the camera lifts toward
   `altar_focus` (0.8 s), a warm cover fades in with "The Tournament begins" (0.9 s), then under the cover Iris is
   teleported to the island `SPAWN`, the island world, kill height, camera and atmosphere are restored and
   `startPlay` runs. The cover waits until every archipelago asset in view from the island spawn is loaded and
   warmed (at most 20 s) and fades out. While Iris walks the town, the archipelago's files are fetched into the
   HTTP cache at low priority (not on Save-Data or 2G/3G links), so that wait is short. Under reduced motion there
   is no flare wait and no camera move: a plain 0.4 s cross-fade.
6. There is no way back to the town in v1. The streamer releases the town's models by itself a few seconds later.

## Assets

- Files: `game/public/assets/models/town/<name>.glb`, optional `<name>_m.glb` phone variants. `virtual:model-list`
  (`game/build/model-list.js`) lists the flat folder and `town/`, and exports `MODEL_DIRS` (name → `'town/'`) so
  `fetchGltf` and the review page resolve the URL. Names are unique across both folders, `[a-z0-9_]`.
- Catalog: `TOWN_ASSETS` in `render/asset-catalog.js` is derived from `TOWN_DATA.pieces`. Kinds and budgets:
  `town_terrain` 120k triangles, `town_block` 120k, `town_hero` 16k runtime / 60k `_inspect`, `town_sky` 20k.
- Town pieces have **no greybox**: nothing is shown and nothing collides before the file arrives. Streaming bounds
  come from `center` and `radius` in the data until the real bounds are known.
- Inside a `.glb`: `*_col` meshes become colliders (never rendered); `socket_lamp_*` empties get up to six warm
  point lights without shadows on the six nearest Iris (they fade as they move); `town_sky` is visual only (its colliders,
  if any, are ignored and it casts no shadow).
- `town_altar.glb`: material `M_Altar_Core` (the crystal) is driven by code: `emissiveIntensity` 0.3 asleep, 1.3
  awake (eased from 2.5 radii down to the prompt radius), a slow pulse (steady under reduced motion), and a flare
  during registration. The authored emissive strength is overwritten; colour is kept. The ring glyphs (nodes
  `town_altar_altar_glyph_00..15`, material `M_Altar_Glyph`, cloned per node) light in sequence as Iris
  approaches, all lit at the stand, at 0.35 of the core; `M_Altar_Eye` follows at 0.1. Without those nodes only
  the core is driven. Empties `altar_focus` and `altar_stand` replace the data's points when present.
- `town_sky` is drawn with fog off (it carries its own painted haze).
- Atmosphere: `LookDev.setAtmosphereFloor` moves the height fog base to `TOWN_ORIGIN.y − 20` and the cloud sea to
  `TOWN_ORIGIN.y − 32` as mist around the hill; the sun, sky and time of day stay. Restored on leaving.
- `tests/client/assets-budget.test.mjs` checks the town folder too. `TOWN_BLOCKOUT_OK` (now `false`: the final files are in) would tolerate an
  uncompressed blockout round.

## Ambient life

`game/src/scenes/town-life.js`, created by the town scene, plus the shared wind of `render/weather.js`:

- **Cloth and plants** (`M_Town_Cloth`, `M_Town_Foliage`) move with the wind. The town files carry no `_WIND`
  attribute, so the client computes the weights when a model arrives (`windWeights` in `render/weather.js`), and
  the rule is that unknown geometry stays still. Connected parts whose boxes touch are one object (a banner and
  its tail, an awning and its stripes). A tall hanging sheet (a banner) is pinned along its upper 55 %, where the
  ring emblem of another material sits, and swings at the hem; a stretched sheet (awning, stall roof) breathes a
  few centimetres at its lower edge; any other cloth (rugs, candles, books) does not move. A tree crown leans as
  one body rooted at its bottom; window boxes and planters shiver. A file that carries `_WIND` keeps its authored
  weights. The first version (11.11, weights per connected part by height alone) let banners swing away from
  their emblems, awning stripes slide apart and small props bend: owner bug report, fixed the same day.
  Hanging shop signs and lamp heads do not swing: they are merged into the block meshes, a rigid swing needs them
  as separate nodes from the art build.
- **Chimney smoke** at every `socket_smoke_*`: one `THREE.Points` draw call for the town, puffs rise 7 m and lean
  downwind with the gusts; 14 puffs on up to 24 chimneys on desktop, 7 on 8 on phones and the low tier.
- **Birds**: 13 (7 on phones) in two loose flocks circling over the roofs, one instanced draw call.
- **Sky islands** (`town_sky`) drift slowly (a 2 m bob, a 5 m sway, an 80 s period).
- **Altar**: core pulse, the sixteen glyphs lighting in sequence as Iris approaches, flare on registration (above).

All of it runs in vertex shaders from one time value; under reduced motion that value stands still (smoke hangs
as still plumes, birds hold their place) and the wind strength is zero.

## Loading and the altar transition

Iris starts `SPAWN_ADVANCE` (4.5 m) up the street from the plan's gate point: right in front of the gate arch the
arch pushed the follow camera into her back.

The loading screen and the altar's cover keep their looks (owner) and share one pair of elements from
`game/src/ui/ink-bar.js`: a **hand-drawn progress bar** and a **log of what is loading**. The bar is a sprite
atlas rendered in Blender (`art/ui/loading_bar/build_bar.py` → `game/public/assets/ui/loading_bar.webp`, 337 KB):
25 rows of progress by 3 columns of the same drawing redrawn with another hand tremor. The row follows real
progress (the loading screen's own value; on the cover the bytes and warm-up of the archipelago's first view), the
column cycles every 190 ms for the line boil and holds still under reduced motion. On the dark loading screen the
bar sits on a parchment plaque, because its ink is the game's warm brown. The log shows the last three lines
(`loading.log.*` keys in all five locales; English until the locale chunk is in), read from the streamer: the
asset in flight with the highest priority, "Warming shaders…" while something is warming. `ink-bar.js` lives in
the `boot` chunk (`game/build/chunks.js`): in the `ui` chunk it closed a chunk cycle and the build did not boot.
`tools/gpu-server/flow-cap.cjs` records the whole start as a frame sequence (loading, first town frames, cover).

## Not in this version

- **Shops are not enterable.** `TOWN_DATA.shops` and `TOWN_DATA.interiors` are data only; the interior files
  (`town_shop_*.glb`) ship in the folder but the client neither lists them in the catalog nor loads them, and
  there is no prompt at the shop doors.
- **Townsfolk** (`TOWN_DATA.npc`) are another lane; none are placed.
- **Well water** (`M_Town_Water`) does not shimmer yet. The rest of the ambient motion is in: see "Ambient life".
- Footsteps and other audio are silent in the town. Light, fog, grade and weather: `docs/atmosphere.md`.
- No way back from the archipelago to the town.

## QA hooks

`window.__ftf.town` (null when the town is skipped):

- `ready`, `active`, `registered`, `phase` (`off`, `town`, `transition`, `done`), `promptVisible`, `walkMs`;
- `register()`: the same code path as pressing `E`; resolves `true` when the season runs, `false` when the prompt
  is not offered;
- `walkRoute()`: steers Iris along the jury route with the player's physics (a blocked route never arrives);
- `status()`: every piece with its streaming state; `scene` (the town handle), `flow`, `plan`.

Tests: `tests/client/town.test.mjs` (layout contract, catalog, `townPlan`, model list) in `npm test`;
`tests/e2e/town.e2e.mjs` (browser: walk, register, archipelago, phone prompt). With `FTF_TOWN_SHOTS=blockout` (or
`v1`) the e2e run saves its screenshots to `docs/art/town/qa/ingame/`.

## The town quest (hackathon spec P1-B)

`game/src/scenes/town-quest.js` (pure, `tests/client/town-quest.test.mjs`) and its UI in `town-flow.js`.
Three townspeople who already stand at fixed posts on the way up teach the loop before the season:

1. **Innkeeper** (lower street, west of the gate road): shows the tutorial's example sign and asks for a reading.
   A wrong answer explains the stroke order and shows the sign again. The right one gives a note for the baker.
2. **Baker** (the square stall): takes the note, gives a 5-silk practice purse ("town coins, nothing on a chain"),
   and trades 2 of it for a sealed word.
3. **Shrinekeeper** (beside the altar): takes the word and sends Iris to register at the altar.

- Steps `start -> read -> trade -> deliver -> done`. The objective line follows the step, then reads "Walk to the
  Tournament altar" again. Within `TALK_RADIUS` (2.4 m) of the townsperson the step waits for, the prompt reads
  "Talk to the …". It takes precedence over the altar prompt (the shrinekeeper stands beside the altar). A talk
  closes on **Later** or when Iris walks more than 4.5 m away; the step stays.
- Played once per device (`ftf.townquest.v1` = `done` or `skipped`). **Skip the town quest** sits under the
  objective. Never on `?demo=judge`, so the README's 5-minute table is unchanged. `?quest=1` forces it (QA),
  `?quest=0` skips it.
- No rule changes: the quest sends no engine command, the purse lives in the town UI only and is labeled "a
  teaching prop, not on-chain", and the altar registers at any step.
