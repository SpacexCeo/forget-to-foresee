# The town on the ground (client)

Play starts in a hill town below the archipelago. Iris walks from the lower gate up the main street to the
Tournament altar, registers, and is carried up to the archipelago, where the season starts exactly as before.
Art brief and acceptance: `docs/art/town-brief.md`. This page is the code side.

No rule changes: `engine.js`, the room and the parity tests are untouched. The town runs before the play layer
exists; it has no world HUD, no hub and no hints.

## Modules

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `game/src/scenes/town-data.js` | Generated by `art/town/` (Blender plan). Town-local metres: `spawn`, `altar {position, stand, focus, radius}`, `route`, `killY`, `pieces [{name, kind, position, center, radius}]`. Never edited by hand. |
| `game/src/scenes/town-layout.js` | Pure helpers over the data: `TOWN_ORIGIN` (`[0, 1200, 0]`), world-space spawn, altar, route, kill height, fog and mist levels, `altarWake`, `inAltarRange`, the flat dev ground. Node-tested. |
| `game/src/scenes/town.js` | `createTownScene({scene, streamer, flat, pointLights})`: its own `CollisionWorld`, one `PropInstance` per piece on the shared `AssetStreamer`, `ready`, the altar core glow, lamp lights, `groundAt`, `setActive`. |
| `game/src/scenes/town-flow.js` | `createTownFlow(...)`: the objective line, the altar prompt, the registration sequence and its cover, the QA route walker. |
| `game/src/boot-world.js` | Decides with `townPlan`, places Iris in the town, holds her until there is ground, routes input, and on leaving restores the island world, kill height, camera and atmosphere, then calls `startPlay`. |
| `game/src/ui/loading.js` | `townPlan(params)`: the URL rule (pure, tested). |
| `game/src/play/iris-avatar.js`, `game/src/play/gait.js` | Iris's model, gait and look-at, split out of `play/agents.js` so she walks the town before the season. The same `IrisAvatar` is handed to `startPlay` (`options.iris`), so her model loads once. |

The town lives in the same `THREE.Scene` as the archipelago, 1200 m above it (the camera far plane is 500 m), as
the Shell interior does at `[0, 300, 0]`. `player.world` and `rig.world` point at the town's collision world
while Iris is there. `CharacterController.killY` is absolute, so it is `TOWN_ORIGIN.y + TOWN_DATA.killY` in the
town and the island's `KILL_Y` afterwards; the last safe point is always inside the town.

## URL parameters

| URL | Result |
| --- | --- |
| *(none)*, `?demo=judge` | Lobby, then the town. This is the default and the jury link. |
| `?skip=town` | Straight to the archipelago. |
| `?play=direct`, `?cinematic=…`, `?view=…`, `?film=…` | Dev and QA deep links: the archipelago as before, no town. |
| `?play=direct&town=1` | The direct QA link into the town (also with `demo=judge`). |
| `?town=altar` | Spawn at the altar stand, facing the altar (screenshots). |
| `?town=flat` | No models: a flat dev ground and a code-drawn altar. Combine as `?town=altar,flat`. |

A phone (coarse pointer) has no lobby and starts in the town directly.

## Flow

1. `createWorld` builds the town when `townPlan` says so, teleports Iris to the spawn and sets the camera behind
   her at 13 m. The archipelago's models are not loaded: they are out of the frustum and the streamer ignores them.
2. There is no ground until `town_terrain` has streamed in. Iris is held (no physics steps) and the objective line
   reads "The town is taking shape…". When the terrain is real, a ray cast from the spawn finds the ground and she
   is set on it. The loading screen of the direct path also waits for this, up to 15 s.
3. The player walks with the normal input: keyboard, click or tap to move, gamepad, Shift or the touch toggle to run.
   The objective reads "Walk to the Tournament altar". Iris's model and gait come from `IrisAvatar`.
4. Inside `altar.radius` of the altar the prompt "Register for the Tournament" appears: key `E`, the gamepad's
   interact button, or a tap on the prompt (it is a button; on touch it shows no key cap).
5. Registration (`TOWN_TRANSITION`, about 3 s): Iris is held, the amber core flares and the camera lifts toward
   `altar_focus` (0.8 s), a warm cover fades in with "The Tournament begins" (0.9 s), then under the cover Iris is
   teleported to the island `SPAWN`, the island world, kill height, camera and atmosphere are restored and
   `startPlay` runs. The cover waits for the island model (at most 6 s) and fades out. Under reduced motion there
   is no flare wait and no camera move: a plain 0.4 s cross-fade.
6. There is no way back to the town in v1. The streamer releases the town's models by itself a few seconds later.

## Assets

- Files: `game/public/assets/models/town/<name>.glb`, optional `<name>_m.glb` phone variants. `virtual:model-list`
  (`game/build/model-list.js`) lists the flat folder and `town/`, and exports `MODEL_DIRS` (name → `'town/'`) so
  `fetchGltf` and the review page resolve the URL. Names are unique across both folders, `[a-z0-9_]`.
- Catalog: `TOWN_ASSETS` in `render/asset-catalog.js` is derived from `TOWN_DATA.pieces`. Kinds and budgets:
  `town_terrain` 120k triangles, `town_block` 120k, `town_hero` 16k runtime / 60k `_inspect`, `town_sky` 20k.
- Town pieces have **no greybox**: nothing is shown and nothing collides before the file arrives. Streaming bounds
  come from `center` and `radius` in the data until the real bounds are known.
- Inside a `.glb`: `*_col` meshes become colliders (never rendered); `socket_lamp_*` empties get up to six warm
  point lights without shadows on the six nearest Iris (they fade as they move); `town_sky` is visual only (its colliders,
  if any, are ignored and it casts no shadow).
- `town_altar.glb`: material `M_Altar_Core` (emissive, with its mask) is driven by code: `emissiveIntensity` 0.3
  asleep, 1.3 awake (eased from 2.5 radii down to the prompt radius), a slow pulse (steady under reduced motion),
  and a flare during registration. The authored emissive strength is overwritten; colour and mask are kept.
  Empties `altar_focus` and `altar_stand` replace the data's points when present.
- Atmosphere: `LookDev.setAtmosphereFloor` moves the height fog base to `TOWN_ORIGIN.y − 20` and the cloud sea to
  `TOWN_ORIGIN.y − 32` as mist around the hill; the sun, sky and time of day stay. Restored on leaving.
- `tests/client/assets-budget.test.mjs` checks the town folder too. `TOWN_BLOCKOUT_OK` (now `false`: the final files are in) would tolerate an
  uncompressed blockout round.

## QA hooks

`window.__ftf.town` (null when the town is skipped):

- `ready`, `active`, `registered`, `phase` (`off`, `town`, `transition`, `done`), `promptVisible`, `walkMs`;
- `register()`: the same code path as pressing `E`; resolves `true` when the season runs, `false` when the prompt
  is not offered;
- `walkRoute()`: steers Iris along the jury route with the player's physics (a blocked route never arrives);
- `status()`: every piece with its streaming state; `scene` (the town handle), `flow`, `plan`.

Tests: `tests/client/town.test.mjs` (layout contract, catalog, `townPlan`, model list) in `npm test`;
`tests/e2e/town.e2e.mjs` (browser: walk, register, archipelago, phone prompt). With `FTF_TOWN_SHOTS=blockout` (or
`v1`) the e2e run saves its screenshots to `docs/art/town/qa/ingame/`.
