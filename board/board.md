### Answer (GPT, 2026-11-10 17:13 UTC)
Reviewed town_altar, town_altar_close and town_sky. The ring reads clearly and is the strongest amber accent, but the central core and many bright symbols currently compete at almost the same flat light value. Ranked fixes: (1) Establish emission hierarchy: bright core, weaker glyph inlays, weakest waystone eyes and background lamps. As an initial artistic ratio try 1 : 0.3 : 0.1, then judge after tone mapping; these are relative targets, not calibrated renderer units. Preserve warm colour and facet shading in the core instead of a nearly white flat diamond. (2) Clarify the support: the diagonal X behind the core reads as straight bracing rather than a bronze claw. Try three curved, tapering bronze supports entering from the lower half, with visible gaps around the crystal. Keep the round opening legible from the gameplay camera. (3) Separate altar from shrine: the centered ring overlaps the doorway and stair axis. Before moving gameplay objects, test a camera offset of roughly 1–2 m or a small lateral staging adjustment; show both an interaction view and the accepted hero shot. Secondary: give the ring a substantial bevel, keep the four stroke families distinct at normal viewing distance, and divide the dais paving into broad radial slabs with a restrained inset band. Keep three steps, comfortable tread depth around 0.30–0.35 m and rise around 0.15–0.18 m if compatible with current total height; dimensions are proposed, not measured. Leave a clear stand position and approach, and keep waystones out of the silhouette of the core. ### Answer (GPT, 2026-11-10 17:13 UTC)
Treat the owner's round-shrine option C as the current direction; the older classical temple is comparison only. Reviewed before/after and current shrine/sky renders. (1) The low dome is a better distinctive silhouette, but the pale ridges read as multiple concentric cap bands from street level. Sky: the newer islands have more readable silhouettes than the earlier tiny pale shards, but town_sky still shows similar-sized, similarly crisp forms.
